# Final homepage (`final`), built on Option B "Night Assembly"

- **Idea:** the Assembly hall after dark, now as one continuous flight: the broadcast opening, then the camera flies from orbit into Dubai while the resolution assembles, and every section after it arrives as the next stop on that route. Same facts, text and liked pieces as Option B (opening, globe, placards, clauses, full top nav, short page, IHS colours).
- **Palette:** B's ground `#0A0F18`, surface `#111826`, hairline `#1C2533`, border `#2B3546`, muted `#8A94A6`, body `#C3C9D3`, white `#F4F5F2`; gold `#FFB606` stays the one accent; **UN blue `#009EDB` is now the only action colour** (buttons, links, tab indicator, checked boxes; 6.3:1 on the ground, ink text on it 6.3:1); **IHS maroon `#600E0E` host strip** with cream `#DCBDAB` text and the gold dot with its maroon ring. Type: Geist 200–500, Geist Mono labels.
- **References and the technique borrowed from each** (techniques only; no assets, copy or layouts; sites not browsed, worked from the design library's notes). Code locations in brackets.
  1. **Igloo Inc** (Site of the Year): one continuous scroll-driven camera move. The pinned opening flies from orbit into Dubai (cobe globe), and each later section arrives like the camera settling on a stop: it rises 110px, scales up from 94% and comes out of the dark, scrubbed to the scroll. A journey rail on the right (desktop ≥1280px) fills as you travel. [`fx.ts` useJourney, `Home.tsx` Journey]
  2. **Lusion v3** (Site of the Year): spring physics. The top placard tilts towards the cursor on a spring (rotateX/rotateY, `motion` useSpring), the deck keeps its drag and velocity-carrying flick, and each floor nameplate has a magnetic spring pull towards the cursor. Mouse only; touch keeps the plain drag and flick. [`Deck.tsx`, `Floor.tsx`]
  3. **Lando Norris** (Site of the Year): a cursor-reactive hero. A soft circular mask hole follows the mouse anywhere over the hero section (the listener is the whole section, not the letters) and shows a gold outline layer beneath "DXB MUN", only inside the circle; it closes on pointerleave. Everyone gets one slow automatic pass on load, which always ends closed (it is jumped to its end if the tab is hidden, or by a timer, so a paused rAF cannot leave a window parked); touch gets only that pass. Checked with real mouse moves by `spot-check.mjs`. [`fx.ts` useSpotlight, `.ident-spot` in `final.css`]
  4. **Opal Tadpole** (product): a scroll-pinned, one-step-at-a-time sequence. On desktop the Schedule pins under the bar and the scroll steps Day 1 → Day 2 → Day 3 (the same coss Tabs, driven by scroll), with a gold progress line, rows stepping in and the day's counts rolling. It uses only the existing schedule data: the suggested "Committee → Debate → Resolution → Vote" labels were not used, because those words are not in the site's facts. [`Home.tsx` Schedule]
  5. **WEIMA** (industrial): colour-block transitions. A top-nav click (and the journey rail, "Explore committees") sends a solid IHS-maroon block with a gold leading edge across the screen; the page jumps under it and the block leaves the other way to reveal the section. [`fx.ts` wipeTo]
  6. **Cartier Watches & Wonders** (luxury): slow, heavy mask reveals. On load the headline rises through a clip-path mask (1.9s, power4.inOut) and the year line wipes in after it; the hero photo opens through a centred window while the slabs peel; the placard deck and the floor-timer card are unmasked top to bottom when they arrive. [`Opening.tsx`, `fx.ts`]
  7. **Mat Voyce** (portfolio): kinetic type. Each clause's verb slides in, then the fact's characters assemble, each rising out of its own mask in reading order, scrubbed to the globe flight (GSAP SplitText 3.15, free since 3.13, `aria-label` keeps the fact readable). Section headings do the same once when they come into view. [`Opening.tsx`, `ui.tsx` Keyed]
  8. **Terminal Industries** (B2B): precise, data-like micro-motion. The Dubai clock's digits roll every second, the schedule counts and the wellness count roll (NumberFlow), and the HUD's lat/lon/zoom track the flight live. [`ui.tsx`, `Home.tsx`]
  9. **Iventions** (events): Lenis smooth scroll driving GSAP ScrollTrigger scrubs (the shared `useSmoothScroll`, scrub 1.2s) for the opening, the journey and the pinned schedule.
- **Performance and access:** only transform, opacity and clip-path animate (the spotlight repaints one masked text layer); the globe's WebGL draws only from its own rAF loop, which stops when the globe is off screen (cobe has no loop of its own). Still mode (`?still`) and prefers-reduced-motion render the calm static page: no pin, no splits, no wipes, no spotlight. Phones: no tilt, no magnetic pull, no rail, no schedule pin; the spotlight plays once on its own.
- **Not used:** `@paper-design/shaders-react` was not installed (no npm install): the cobe globe is already the page's WebGL layer, and a second WebGL context would cost RAM and battery on phones for a backdrop the dark ground does not need.
