# Merged (`merged`)

- **Idea:** one serious world-news broadcast on deep navy (`#07111F`) with UN blue `#009EDB` as the only accent. It merges four owner favourites: Live's opening, Orbit's globe zooming to Dubai, Resolution's clauses and Placard's nameplates. Everything shares one navy scale, square corners, 1px rules and one reveal motif (the UN-blue "key" bar). Type: Barlow Condensed (display and UI), Inter Tight (text), Instrument Serif italic (the resolution verbs only), and Geist Mono (data).
- **Which Live opening:** the ORIGINAL title card from the published preview (`site/preview/`, built before Live's round-3 edits): a huge "DXB MUN" over a UN-blue 2027 block on navy slabs, wiped open onto the full photo. The round-3 rules sit on top of it. The title card is the `h1`, and the one-line description, Dates/Venue, "Explore committees" and the teaser sit under it, all visible on the first screen at 390, 820 and 1440. The mechanics come from the current `concepts/broadcast` source: shared scroll feel, progress line, the quieter corner tag, scroll-spy and the burger sheet.
- **Signature (one pinned track, 300svh, `Opening.tsx`):** one GSAP timeline (scrub = `SCROLL_FEEL.scrub`) and the globe's own rAF loop read the same scroll progress.
  - **A (0–0.27, 60% of a viewport):** the blue and white sweeps cross, the title card is pushed off, six slabs peel away, the photo settles from 1.18×, and the lower-third keys on.
  - **B:** a hold on the photo.
  - **C:** a navy band with a UN-blue edge wipes across and hands the photo over to the globe.
  - **D (0.40–0.97):** the cobe globe turns 385°, tilts and zooms to Dubai, with a HUD, a reticle and a "DUBAI" tag with the live Asia/Dubai clock. Meanwhile the resolution panel slides in (from the left on desktop, from the bottom below 1024px) and each clause inks in word by word over 0.6 of a viewport.
  - **"About" in the nav** lands at the end of the pin, where every clause has inked in.
- **Resolution, skimmable:** a kicker, then an h2 "A Model United Nations in Dubai", then 6 clauses. Each clause is an italic verb, one large fact and a supporting line of 12 words or fewer: *Hosted by* INDIAN HIGH SCHOOL, *Guided by* DIPLOMACY AROUND US, *Convenes* 19 COMMITTEES, *Meets for* 3 DAYS · 6 SESSIONS, *Dates* TO BE ANNOUNCED, *Invites you to* EXPLORE COMMITTEES → (a link). All facts come from `conference.ts`.
- **Committees:** Placard's throwable deck, with Motion drag, inertia, `touch-action: none`, arrow keys, buttons, Shuffle and a NumberFlow counter. Beside it is the floor of 19 nameplates that rise row by row on scroll (5 columns on desktop, 4 on phones), and any plate calls its committee to the top of the deck. The nameplates are restyled: off-white with navy Barlow capitals and a UN-blue header bar.
- **Rest:** Schedule uses day tabs with a colour key, and the duration is dropped on phones. Quick reset and Resources (with the floor-timer promo and the portal links) share one asymmetric row. Contact is a full-bleed deep-brown band, followed by the maroon host strip and a slim footer. (Before v2, Contact was a third column in that row.)
- **Tools:** Live's shot-clock timer (seven-segment SVG, 40-cell bar, Space/R, `aria-live`, full screen, red "Time is up") and the control-room portals, restyled with Inter Tight for message text. Both are lazy-loaded and share one `usePortal()` store.
- **Still / reduced motion:** the opening renders its last photo frame with the h1 in the lower-third. Below it, a static section shows the SVG globe landed on Dubai beside the fully inked resolution. Nothing is pinned.
- **Heights (motion):** 4587px at 1440 (5.1 screens), 6908px at 820 (5.9) and 5497px at 390 (6.5).
- **Audit:** no high issues. The only med issue is the known false positive "timer numerals 0px" (the digits are SVG).
- **Libraries:** gsap + ScrollTrigger + @gsap/react, lenis (via `@/shared/scroll`), cobe (lazy), motion/react, @number-flow/react, coss ui on @base-ui/react (see LIBRARY.md), Tailwind 4 theme + utilities (no preflight), lucide-react, clsx. No new npm packages in v2.
- **v2 warm hints (2026-11-05, owner: "add hints of orange and brown").** The tokens sit in `merged.css` next to the IHS tokens. They are accents only: navy stays the base and UN blue stays the only action colour. Contrast is WCAG 2 relative luminance, computed.

  | Token | Value | Where it is used | Contrast notes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `--orange` (burnt orange) | `#C8642A` | clause numbers (text), resolution reading rule, ticker separators, section-head rule segment, nav hover underline, tag borders (coss Badge), "To be announced" marker square, floor-timer card hover border, contact band top rule, sheet title dash | Text only on navy `#07111F`: 4.79:1. It is never text on brown (3.66:1), on `--g1` (4.18:1) or on maroon (3.39:1), and never a button, a link or a fill |
  | `--brown` (deep brown) | `#3B2418` | the Contact band (the one warm surface, directly above the maroon host strip); desk tops under the nameplates; the hard drop under the placard deck | On brown: white 13.5:1, cream `#DCBDAB` 8.2:1, `--g5` 9.4:1, `--g4` 5.6:1, UN-blue link 4.8:1 |
  | `--brown-2` (mid brown) | `#6B4430` | placard edges (1px ring), the placard fold rule, nameplate inner edge, desk edges, row rules inside the Contact band | Line colour only (7.6:1 against the plate, 2.3:1 against navy, decorative) |

- **v2 library sweep.** Every generic control is now coss ui (Button, Menu, Sheet, Badge, Tabs, Checkbox, Card, Toggle Group, Number Field), themed through the coss variables. Wellness and Resources share an asymmetric row, and Contact is a brown colophon band. See `LIBRARY.md` for the full sweep and the sources.
- **Gaps:** the main JS chunk is about 734 kB (246 kB gzip). On phones the globe gets only the top ~200px during the resolution. Between 1024 and 1100px the "Explore committees" clause can wrap to two lines.
