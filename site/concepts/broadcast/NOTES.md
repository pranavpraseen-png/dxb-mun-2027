# Live (`broadcast`)

- **Idea:** DXB MUN 2027 as a serious world-news broadcast package, in the spirit of UN Web TV. The ground is deep navy (`#07111F`), with UN blue `#009EDB` as the only accent and white type. Green, amber and red appear only as portal status colours, and red is used for "Time is up". Type is Barlow Condensed set upright at 500–700, with Geist Mono for data. The graphics are lower-thirds, a calm ticker, a quiet "DXB MUN 2027" corner bug, an "At a glance" stats board, channels, a TV guide and a shot clock.
- **First screen (before any scroll):** a lower-third with the name (h1), "Model United Nations, Dubai", the one-line description, Dates and Venue ("To be announced") and ONE primary button, "Explore committees". It also carries a teaser ("19 committees, 3 days") and the ticker. On desktop the right half shows a picture-in-picture preview of the photo, and on phones the preview sits above the lower-third.
- **Signature:** `Intro.tsx`, a broadcast-intro wipe scrubbed by scroll (GSAP ScrollTrigger, `scrub` = SCROLL_FEEL.scrub × 1.1, on a 165vh pinned stage, so the wipe runs over 65% of a viewport). A UN-blue bar and a white bar sweep across, the preview window dissolves, and six diagonal navy slabs peel away alternately up and down to open the full photo, which settles from 1.18× to 1×. With `?still` or reduced motion, only the final frame renders.
- **Scroll and nav:** `useSmoothScroll(true, 60)` from `@/shared/scroll` (no CSS smooth scrolling). Every nav jump goes through `scrollToSection`. The desktop nav shows every section plus Timer and the Portal menu, with a scroll-spy highlight and a 2px UN-blue progress line under the bar. On phones and tablets, a full-screen menu.
- **Home:** intro → motto plus a NumberFlow board with the "To be announced" facts → committees as 19 channels (CH+/CH− buttons, arrow, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End and number keys to type a channel, swipe, Random, a scaleY channel-switch, and a one-row channel strip on phones) → TV-guide schedule (day tabs, a proportional timeline strip and a listing) → Quick reset, a timer promo and resources → contact credits.
- **Timer:** the shot clock uses seven-segment SVG numerals and a 40-cell depletion bar. The tags are Ready, Running (blue) and Paused, and the screen turns red at "Time is up". Space starts and pauses, R resets, there is a full-screen button, and the state is announced through `aria-live`. The audit's "numerals 0px" is a false reading: the digits are SVG and fill about 470px of height at 1440×900.
- **Portals:** a control-room layout with monitor panels, tally counters and status pills (amber = waiting, green = delivered, red = not approved), plus a lower-third toast. Both portals share one `usePortal()` instance and are lazy-loaded.
- **Libraries:** gsap + ScrollTrigger + @gsap/react, lenis (through `@/shared/scroll`), motion/react, @number-flow/react, @base-ui/react (Menu and Dialog), lucide-react and clsx.
- **Known gaps:** the main JS chunk is about 225 kB gzipped. I did not screenshot the "Time is up" and toast states.
