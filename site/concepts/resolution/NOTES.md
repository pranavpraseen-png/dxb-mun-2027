# Resolution (night edition)

- **Idea:** the homepage is typeset as a UN draft resolution set in reverse, like an official document's night edition. Deep navy ground (#0A1A30), off-white type, UN blue (#009EDB) as the only signal, a strict 12-column grid and hairline rules. A document header carries Distr., Host and Committees, with Dates and Venue "To be announced". The motto "Diplomacy around us" is the resolution's title. Italic preambulatory and numbered operative clauses state only facts from `conference.ts`. Annex I holds the committees, Annex II the schedule, Annex III the quick reset and tools, and the page closes with "Done at Dubai, on to be announced."
- **Type:** Instrument Serif (display, clauses, placards, timer), Inter Tight (text and UI), JetBrains Mono (clause numbers, labels and typed chits).
- **Scroll feel (round 3):** `useSmoothScroll(true, 64)` from `@/shared/scroll` on the homepage, with no CSS smooth scroll. Every nav link, hero button and margin note goes through `scrollToSection`. A 2px UN-blue scroll-progress line sits under the nav.
- **Signature:** each clause inks in word by word, from a faint ghost to full off-white, scrubbed by scroll (GSAP ScrollTrigger with `scrub: SCROLL_FEEL.scrub`, opacity only). Each clause takes 62% of a viewport to fully ink (top 96% to top 34%). A UN-blue reading rule fills down the margin, and the "See Annex" margin notes appear as their clause completes. Still mode and reduced motion render everything inked.
- **First screen:** "Explore committees" is the one primary button, with "About the conference" as secondary. The name, host, city, dates/venue "To be announced" and the primary button are all visible at 390×844, 820×1180 and 1440×900, and the desktop hero has a scroll cue.
- **Committees:** a compact two-column index of all 19 with even 50px rows, beside a large off-white tent placard. Hovering or focusing a row updates it. At 899px and below it becomes a picker: the placard on top and a 4-column grid of committee codes.
- **Nav:** every section plus Timer and Portal (a Base UI Menu with Delegate and Chair) is visible at 1024px and up. Scroll-spy underlines the current section, and anchors scroll smoothly (instantly in still mode). Phones get a full-screen menu (Base UI Dialog) with 56px rows.
- **Timer:** enormous serif numerals in fixed-width cells, a blue progress hairline and an italic state line in `aria-live`. The page turns alarm red when time is up. Space and R work, and there is a full-screen button.
- **Portals:** chits are set as typed memos. Status pills use semantic colours (amber waiting, green delivered, red not approved), and the stats use NumberFlow. One shared `usePortal()` store links the chair and delegate views. Timer and portals are lazy-loaded.
- **Libraries:** gsap + ScrollTrigger + @gsap/react, @base-ui/react (Menu, Dialog), @number-flow/react and the @fontsource fonts. Motion and Lenis are not used.
- **Heights:** home is 4576px at 1440 (5.1 viewports), 5552px at 820 (4.7) and 5264px at 390 (6.2). `audit.mjs` reports no issues.
- **Gaps:** desktop shows all three schedule days side by side, because it is no taller than a single tab; phones use day tabs. The main JS chunk is about 507 kB (170 kB gzip). The "DXB/2027/L.1" document code was removed in round 3.
