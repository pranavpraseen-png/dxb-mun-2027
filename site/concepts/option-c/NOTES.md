# Option C: IHS Maroon (`option-c`)

- **Idea:** the host school's own conference, laid out like a ceremony programme. It keeps Merged's broadcast opening, the globe zoom to Dubai, the resolution clauses, the placard deck and the full top nav, and the sections alternate as full-bleed bands (cream, deep maroon, cream, IHS maroon) with small gold numerals I to V.
- **Palette (`maroon.css`):** ground `#2A0707`, IHS maroon `#600E0E` for bands, panels and desks, IHS cream `#DCBDAB` for secondary text, light cream `#F4E9E0` for primary text and the cream bands, IHS gold `#FFB606` for honours only (the year, clause numbers, the host pip, the chosen nameplate, numerals), and UN blue `#009EDB` for buttons and links only (`#0069A6` on cream, which is 4.9:1). No orange and no brown.
- **Type:** Playfair Display 800–900 for headings and the ident (caps), Playfair italic for the clause verbs, Archivo for body text and tracked caps labels, and Geist Mono for the HUD, the clock and the times.
- **References and borrowed techniques (sites not browsed; worked from the award notes):** Layout: Designex by Dokmeh for big display serif titles with small gold numerals and a short dek; Iventions for event structure as full-bleed bands, one per part of the programme; Silver Pinewood Residences (Vide Infra) for even, generous band padding. Motion personality, confident colour blocks: each band arrives under a solid block of the opposite house colour that sweeps off to the right once (WEIMA's colour-block transitions; `motion.ts`, one scaleX per band); each clause is keyed on by a cream block that wipes across its line with the fact behind it (Mat Voyce's kinetic type, as colour blocks); the globe stays the scroll-pinned zoom into Dubai (Igloo Inc). Lenis smooth scroll as in Merged; still mode and prefers-reduced-motion show final states.
