# Majlis (round 3: calm gate, shared scroll feel)

- **Idea:** a solemn diplomatic hall at night. Navy ground (#07111F to #15304F), UN blue #009EDB for edges and key lines, off-white text. Brass (#B89A5E) is a rare highlight: the Arabic line and the ceremony stars. Green, amber and red are used only for portal and timer status. Type is Fraunces Variable + Manrope + Noto Kufi Arabic, used only for نموذج الأمم المتحدة and دبي (`lang="ar" dir="rtl"`).
- **First screen:** an arched plaque over the closed screens answers the first-5-seconds questions without scrolling. It carries the h1 "DXB MUN 2027", "Model United Nations in Dubai / Hosted by Indian High School", "Dates and venue: To be announced", the primary "Explore committees" button, and the teaser "19 committees over 3 days".
- **Signature:** the two mashrabiya screens (navy at 80% opacity, low-contrast strokes, all star holes open so the photo reads through; UN blue only on the door stiles and the arch edge) part as you scroll. It is a pinned GSAP timeline with scrub 1.4 and a pin of +65% of a viewport. The screens stay as a lattice border and the photo settles from a slow zoom behind the plaque. In `?still`/reduced motion the hero renders open with no pin.
- **Lattice (`lattice.ts`):** a 4.8.8 octagon/square tiling with Hankin polygons-in-contact (67.5°), continuous across tiles.
- **Scroll:** `useSmoothScroll(true, 64)` from `@/shared/scroll`; every nav jump goes through the shared `scrollToSection`. A thin UN-blue progress line sits under the nav. The desktop nav shows every section plus Timer and Portal (a Base UI Menu), with an active-section highlight. The phone header is the wordmark plus a burger that opens a full-screen Base UI Dialog menu.
- **Home:** hero, About (motto, stats frieze, arched particulars card), Committees (one compact snap row of 19 arched niches with a NumberFlow counter), Schedule (Base UI Tabs), Between sessions (wellness, resources, tool links), Contact.
- **Views:** the timer is a huge Fraunces countdown in a pointed arch, and the screen turns deep red for "Time is up". The portals are dense navy panes that share one `usePortal` store; the timer and portals are lazy-loaded.
- **Libraries:** gsap + ScrollTrigger + @gsap/react, lenis (through the shared hook), motion, @number-flow/react, @base-ui/react, lucide-react.
- **Known gaps:** the main JS chunk is about 700 kB (235 kB gzip). The niches only hold an abbreviation and a name. With motion the home page is 5.4 screens at 1440, 5.7 at 820 and 7.7 at 390. `audit.mjs` reports no issues.
