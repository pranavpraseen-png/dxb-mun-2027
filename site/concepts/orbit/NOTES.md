# Orbit

- **Idea:** precise, institutional data-and-diplomacy design. Deep navy (#07111f, with a navy scale for surfaces and lines), UN blue #009EDB as the only accent, 1px lines, Geist + Geist Mono. Mono is kept for real data: coordinates, times, counts. Green, amber and red appear only as status colours in the portals and timer.
- **Signature:** a dotted globe pinned behind the hero and About. Scrolling turns it 385°, tilts it to 25.2048 N and zooms it toward Dubai. When it lands, a reticle and a tag with the live Dubai clock (Asia/Dubai) appear. The globe trails the scroll with a `SCROLL_FEEL.scrub` (1.2 s) lag. The HUD shows "target Dubai", and the live lat/lon/zoom readout fades in only once the approach starts. The About stats roll in with NumberFlow. The pin adds about 0.6 of a screen of scroll.
- **Globe:** `cobe` (WebGL) is lazy-loaded with `import()`. The fallback is a drawn SVG globe made from cobe's own land mask (`landmask.ts`) and projected in `geo.ts`. It is used in still mode, under reduced motion and when WebGL is missing. One pose model drives both renderers; cobe sizes its globe from the canvas height.
- **Committees:** an orbit index with 3 rings (5, 7 and 7 nodes) for all 19. The selected node's ring turns to 12 o'clock while the labels counter-rotate so they stay upright. It works by pointer, keyboard, tap and swipe. "All 19 committees" is a real disclosure: a button with `aria-expanded`, and the list is `hidden` while collapsed, so it pushes the content below it down when open.
- **Scroll and nav:** the shared `useSmoothScroll(true, 64)`; nav, hero buttons and the phone menu jump through `scrollToSection`. A UN-blue progress line runs along the bottom of the nav, and scroll-spy marks the current section. Every section plus Timer and Portal is visible on desktop; phones get a full-screen menu.
- **Timer:** an instrument dial with NumberFlow numerals (about 155px at 1440). Space starts and pauses, R resets, and the state is announced in an `aria-live` region; "Time is up." is a red bar. **Portals:** a dense Linear-style shell labelled "Preview · sample data".
- **Libraries:** cobe, @number-flow/react, motion, @base-ui/react (Menu, Dialog, Tabs), lucide-react, Geist and Geist Mono, plus the shared Lenis/GSAP scroll from `@/shared/scroll`.
- **Audit:** the only finding left is "timer numerals 13px". This is a false reading: the dial digits compute to 155px, but they are NumberFlow (shadow DOM) and the audit seems to pick up the 13px header clock instead. Heights: 4.9 screens at 1440, 5.6 at 820, 7.0 at 390.
- **Known gaps:** the main JS chunk is about 733 kB (243 kB gzipped). It includes GSAP from the shared scroll; lazy-loading Timer and Portal was dropped because dynamic chunks failed intermittently on the dev server. At full zoom, cobe's dots are soft-edged.
