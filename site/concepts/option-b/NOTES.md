# Option B, "Night Assembly" (`option-b`)

- **Idea:** the Assembly hall after dark. One quiet charcoal page with white type, hairline rules and a lot of air. ONE accent, IHS gold, marks only numbers, the year, Dubai, the active nav item and the active nameplate. Every section sits beside a mono index column ("02 Committees") taken from the top nav. The opening, globe, clauses, placards and timer keep Merged's mechanics.
- **Palette:** ground `#0A0F18`, surface `#111826`, hairline `#1C2533`, border `#2B3546`, muted `#8A94A6` (6.3:1), body `#C3C9D3`, white `#F4F5F2` (primary buttons, white on ground 17.6:1), gold `#FFB606` (the only accent, also the focus ring). IHS maroon `#600E0E` appears only as the ring of the gold host pip. There is no orange, no brown and no blue fill. Red `#C8102E` is only for the timer's "Time is up".
- **Type:** Geist Variable, 200–300 for display (very large, sentence case) and 400–500 for text and buttons. Geist Mono, uppercase and tracked at 0.12em, for small labels: clause verbs, indexes, the ticker, the HUD and times.
- **References and borrowed techniques (sites not browsed; worked from the award notes):** Layout: Silver Pinewood Residences (Vide Infra) for premium pacing (very large light headings, wide margins, long quiet gaps); Designex by Dokmeh for the editorial mono index column beside wide content; Iventions for event structure. Motion personality, slow and cinematic: a longer scroll-pinned globe flight into Dubai (360svh pin instead of 300svh, after Igloo Inc's scroll-navigated 3D); each clause rises out of the dark as one title card (Mat Voyce, slowed); every section drifts up out of the dark, scrubbed to the scroll with the opening's weighted lag (`motion.ts`, transform and opacity only). Lenis smooth scroll as in Merged; still mode and prefers-reduced-motion show final states.
