# Option A, "UN Paper" (`option-a`)

- **Idea:** the homepage as a printed UN document: off-white paper, navy ink, double rules like a resolution's header, and a centred document column. The opening, globe (a light "atlas" cobe globe), clauses (preambular verbs in italic, operative verbs underlined), placards and timer keep Merged's mechanics. The one dark band is the IHS maroon host strip, with a gold top rule.
- **Palette:** paper `#F6F3EC`, paper shade `#EDE8DC`, sheet `#FDFCF8`, navy ink `#0F2340`, ink-2 `#33415A` (9.6:1), ink-3 `#5A6577` (5.4:1), hairline `#D6D1C4`, border `#B4AE9F`, deep UN blue `#0B5FA5` (the only action colour, 6.0:1 on paper), IHS maroon `#600E0E` (clause numbers, current nav item, tags, the host strip), gold `#FFB606` (rules and edges only, never text), cream `#DCBDAB` (text on maroon). Red `#B3132B` is only for the timer's "Time is up". No orange, no brown, no shadows, square corners.
- **Type:** Fraunces (variable, opsz) for headings, the ident, clauses and nameplate names; Instrument Sans for text, buttons and small tracked capitals; JetBrains Mono for document symbols, times and data. Nameplate abbreviations are Instrument Sans Bold, like real UN plates.
- **References and borrowed techniques (sites not browsed; worked from the award notes):** Layout: Designex by Dokmeh for the editorial column (oversized serif titles against small tracked running heads, rules instead of boxes); Silver Pinewood Residences (Vide Infra) for the calm, even gap before each section; Iventions for event structure (what, where, when first, then the programme, then the desk). Motion personality, restrained and editorial: headings rise out of a line mask and rows arrive once in reading order (`motion.ts`, GSAP ScrollTrigger.batch, transform and opacity only); clauses are kinetic, each word rising out of its line as the globe lands (Mat Voyce); the globe stays the scroll-pinned zoom into Dubai (Igloo Inc). Lenis smooth scroll as in Merged; still mode and prefers-reduced-motion show final states.
